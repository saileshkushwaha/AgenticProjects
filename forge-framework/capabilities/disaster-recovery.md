# Disaster Recovery Capability

## Definition
The ability to plan and implement disaster recovery strategies.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can follow DR procedures, participate in tests |
| 2 | Competent | Can contribute to DR planning, execute runbooks |
| 3 | Proficient | Can design DR strategies, lead DR tests |
| 4 | Expert | Can design enterprise DR, business continuity |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational DR practices |

## Scope
**In Scope**: BIA, RTO/RPO, backup strategy, recovery procedures, testing
**Out of Scope**: Physical safety, insurance (consult specialists)

## Skills
- **BIA**: Critical system identification, impact analysis
- **Strategy**: Backup, replication, failover design
- **Documentation**: Runbooks, procedures, contact lists
- **Testing**: Tabletop exercises, full DR tests

## Quality Criteria
- BIA complete
- RTO/RPO defined
- Recovery procedures documented
- Testing scheduled
