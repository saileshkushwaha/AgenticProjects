# Retrospective Capability

## Definition
The ability to conduct effective retrospectives that identify improvements and drive continuous learning.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can participate in retrospectives |
| 2 | Competent | Can facilitate basic retrospectives |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead retrospectives and drive improvements |
| 4 | Expert | Can design retrospective processes |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational learning practices |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Sprint retrospectives, project postmortems, incident reviews, continuous improvement
**Out of Scope**: HR performance reviews (consult HR)

## Skills
- **Facilitation**: Leading retrospective sessions
- **Analysis**: Root cause identification
- **Action Planning**: Creating measurable improvements
- **Communication**: Sharing findings and driving adoption

## Assessment Criteria
- [ ] Can facilitate a retrospective
- [ ] Can identify root causes
- [ ] Can create actionable improvements
- [ ] Can drive adoption of changes
- [ ] Can design retrospective processes

## Quality Criteria
- Both positives and negatives documented
- Root causes identified
- Action items have owners and due dates
- Report published and shared
