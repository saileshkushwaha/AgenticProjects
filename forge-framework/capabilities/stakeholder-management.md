# Stakeholder Management Capability

## Definition
The ability to identify, analyze, and engage stakeholders effectively.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can identify stakeholders, follow communication plans |
| 2 | Competent | Can analyze stakeholders, engage effectively |
| 3 | Proficient | Can design engagement strategies, manage conflicts |
| 4 | Expert | Can design enterprise stakeholder programs |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational practices |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Identification, analysis, engagement, communication
**Out of Scope**: HR matters, legal disputes (consult specialists)

## Skills
- **Identification**: Stakeholder mapping, interest analysis
- **Engagement**: Communication, relationship building
- **Conflict Resolution**: Negotiation, mediation
- **Communication**: Tailored messaging, reporting

## Quality Criteria
- Stakeholders identified
- Analysis complete
- Engagement strategy defined
- Communication plan created
