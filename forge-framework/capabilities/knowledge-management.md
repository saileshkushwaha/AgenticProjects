# Knowledge Management Capability

## Definition
The ability to capture, organize, and share organizational knowledge.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can contribute to knowledge base, follow guidelines |
| 2 | Competent | Can create and organize content |
| 3 | Proficient | Can design knowledge programs, drive adoption |
| 4 | Expert | Can design enterprise knowledge strategies |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational practices |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Knowledge base, lessons learned, onboarding, documentation
**Out of Scope**: Training programs (consult L&D)

## Skills
- **Audit**: Gap identification, content cataloging
- **Creation**: Writing, diagramming, recording
- **Organization**: Tagging, search, structure
- **Maintenance**: Review cadence, updates

## Quality Criteria
- Knowledge gaps identified
- Content created and organized
- Maintenance process defined
- Adoption tracked
