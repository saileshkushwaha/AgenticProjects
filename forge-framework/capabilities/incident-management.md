# Incident Management Capability

## Definition
The ability to respond to, resolve, and learn from production incidents.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can follow incident response procedures |
| 2 | Competent | Can triage incidents, implement mitigations |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead incident response, conduct postmortems |
| 4 | Expert | Can design incident management processes |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational incident management |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Detection, classification, mitigation, resolution, postmortem, prevention
**Out of Scope**: Legal/PR response (consult specialists)

## Skills
- **Detection**: Monitoring, alerting, triage
- **Response**: Mitigation, communication, coordination
- **Investigation**: Log analysis, root cause, timeline
- **Learning**: Postmortem, preventive measures, runbook updates

## Quality Criteria
- Incident classified and mitigated
- Root cause identified
- Postmortem completed
- Preventive measures implemented
