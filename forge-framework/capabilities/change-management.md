# Change Management Capability

## Definition
The ability to manage change requests and assess their impact.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can submit change requests, follow procedures |
| 2 | Competent | Can assess impact, document changes |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead change process, manage approvals |
| 4 | Expert | Can design change management programs |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational practices |

## Scope
**In Scope**: Change requests, impact assessment, approval, implementation
**Out of Scope**: Major strategic decisions (executive responsibility)

## Skills
- **Assessment**: Impact analysis, risk assessment
- **Documentation**: Change requests, approval records
- **Communication**: Stakeholder notification, training
- **Tracking**: Implementation monitoring, lessons learned

## Quality Criteria
- Change documented
- Impact assessed
- Approval obtained
- Review complete
