# Compliance Capability

## Definition
The ability to assess and ensure regulatory compliance.

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Title | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Novice | Can follow compliance procedures |
| 2 | Competent | Can conduct basic compliance assessments |
| 3 | Proficient | Can lead compliance audits, gap analysis |
| 4 | Expert | Can design compliance programs |
| 5 | Master | Can establish organizational compliance |

## Scope
**In Scope**: GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2, PCI-DSS, controls, gap analysis, remediation
**Out of Scope**: Legal interpretation (consult legal)

## Skills
- **Assessment**: Control mapping, gap analysis
- **Evidence**: Documentation, interviewing
- **Remediation**: Roadmap creation, tracking
- **Reporting**: Compliance reports, executive summaries

## Quality Criteria
- Controls assessed
- Gaps identified
- Remediation planned
- Evidence documented
